Wallp

An iPhone wallpaper app where artists and photographers published their work and people set it as their wallpaper. A side project built with a developer friend, from the first sketch to the App Store.

Role
Co-founder & Designer
Timeline
2017 — 2020
Platform
iOS
Stack
Sketch, Swift

Background

Egor, a developer friend, and I decided to build a wallpaper app for iPhone. We weren’t out to make anything revolutionary. The goal was experience: build something that works and get it into the App Store.

We had two MacBooks, $2 a month for hosting, a focus group of five, a developer account and plenty of free time.

First version

The MVP was as simple as it gets: a grid of categories like Nature, Cities and Flowers, a full-screen preview with the lock screen clock on top, and one button to save the wallpaper.

The app earned on ads. A Premium collection unlocked the best wallpapers and removed the ads.

A platform for artists

Later Wallp grew from a set of pictures into a platform. Artists and photographers could publish their own collections, and the catalog was split into authors and categories. Each author got a profile with their work.

Outcome

We closed the project in 2020. Wallp never set out to be a business, and it gave us what we wanted: the full path from an idea to a live App Store app with real users.
